Watch the blocker. Make the call.

How to read it

1. Eyes on the blocker first. Are they staying up or pulling off? Which way? Then check: is anyone still moving?

2. Paint the picture. Blocker stays up → "High line" / "High cross" (the side they're taking, so your hitter can go over it). Blocker leaves → "Pulling line" / "Pulling cross". Someone still moving → "Swing line" / "Swing cross" at them. Your hitter still picks the shot.

3. Never say "line" or "cross" alone. Always pair it ("high line", "pulling cross") so your hitter knows exactly what you mean.

When: glance after your set leaves your hands, call as the ball peaks — before your hitter leaves the ground. Repeat it if there's time: "PULLING CROSS! PULLING CROSS!"

What each call opens for your hitter

High line (blocker on line, digger deep cross) → shoot high line (or cutty)

High line, digger creeping up in the cross → high line over the block

High cross (blocker on cross, digger deep line) → cutty (or high cross)

High cross, digger creeping up on the line → high cross over the block (or cutty)

Pulling line, blocker pulled deep → pokey short (or seam)

Pulling line, blocker pulled short → high line deep over them

Pulling line, digger cheating to the seam → cutty

Pulling cross, blocker pulled deep → pokey short middle (or cutty, seam)

Pulling cross, blocker pulled short → high cross deep over them (or seam)

No one (both back), split wide → seam (or pokey, cutty)

No one (both back), close together → pokey (or cutty)

No one (both back), both crept up → jumbo to deep middle

Pick on the pulling blocker: if they pull deep, go short; if they pull short, go deep.

Someone still moving (blocker still pulling, or digger still running) → Swing line / Swing cross toward their side — blast it at them. This is the only call that tells your hitter what to do.

Every correct shot lands in open space — outside the reach rings and block shadow. Swings at a set defender are left out on purpose while you learn calling. The digger takes their standard lane opposite the blocker — only their depth (or a cheat toward the seam) changes. No ball blocks or fakes.
